### Top Of The Funnel

- Increase visibility. Make people (your target audience) know that you exist
- Organic traffic and Engagement: you want to attract the right audience. Make ***relevant*** content
- “TOFU” Metrics:
    - Social Media engagement + Percentage of new sessions
    - Web traffic
    - “Bounce rate”
    - Traffic per channel
    
    (^ setup **Google Analytics**)
    
    ** Don’t fixate too much on numbers, but rather your *success* throughout the Funnel. Also, beware that different types of websites may have different metrics based on their nature
